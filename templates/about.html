Our Story

Germany is globally renowned for its world-class higher education system, cutting-edge research facilities, and remarkable tuition-free policy for both domestic and international students. Yet, every semester, thousands of qualified applicants are rejected simply due to technicalities: missing ECTS prerequisites, wrongly formatted CVs, or Statement of Purpose essays written for the American rather than the German academic system.

EasyWay Germany was started by a collective of international students and graduates from top German universities including TU Munich (TUM), RWTH Aachen, and Heidelberg University. Having overcome the complex maze of uni-assist, German grade conversion, APS verification, and embassy appointments ourselves, we created EasyWay Germany to give applicants the exact digital blueprints, verified university lists, and frameworks needed to succeed independently.
